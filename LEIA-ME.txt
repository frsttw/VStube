Vsy ytd — quick start

1. Open Vsy ytd from the Start menu.
2. Paste one or more YouTube links, one per line.
3. Choose Video and the desired resolution, or Audio only in MP3/M4A.
4. Choose the destination folder.
5. Click "Download now" or press Ctrl+Enter.

In Preferences, configure playlists and whether the destination opens when a batch completes.
In Activity, follow the download and conversion log.

Use the application only for content you own, content in the public domain, or content you are authorized to download. The application does not bypass protections or access private content.

Vsy ytd remembers the folder, format, quality, and preferences.
The installer includes yt-dlp, FFmpeg, FFprobe, and Deno; no separate installation is required.
